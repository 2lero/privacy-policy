RemoteKingdom Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

RemoteKingdom is a remote desktop app for Android TV and Google TV that connects to the RemoteKingdom server running on your own computer. This privacy policy explains what data the app handles and why.

1. Data we collect

The app does not collect any personal data and does not send any data to the app's developer or to third parties. It uses no analytics, advertising or tracking services.

2. Data stored on the device

Only the following is stored locally on the TV, in the app's own storage:
- the server address and port, and the user name entered on the connection screen,
- app settings (e.g. starting Steam, showing statistics),
- the server certificate fingerprint, used to recognise the server on later connections,
- the pairing token received from your server after entering the PIN,
- screenshots taken with SELECT + START, until they are sent to your server.
You can delete this data by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

3. Connection to the server

The app connects only to the server whose address you enter. During a session the following is exchanged between the TV and that server: the desktop picture, sound, input (remote, keyboard, mouse, gamepad) and, if you take them, screenshots. The connection is encrypted (TLS). The server runs on your computer and is not a service operated by the app's developer.

4. Device permissions

The app uses:
- Internet / local network: to connect to your server,
- vibration: to rumble a gamepad connected to the TV when a game uses it.

5. Cookies and tracking

The app does not use cookies or tracking technologies.

6. Data security

Because the app sends no data to the developer or third parties, your data stays on your TV and your computer. The connection to the server is encrypted.

7. Changes to this policy

We may change this privacy policy. Any changes will be published in this file.
