Marina Panic Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Marina Panic is a casual boat docking game for all ages, including children. This privacy policy explains what data the game handles and why.

1. Data we collect

The game does not collect any personal data and does not send any data to the game's developer or to third parties. It uses no analytics, advertising or tracking services, and it does not need an account.

2. Data stored on the device

Only your game progress is stored locally on the device, in the game's own storage: unlocked levels, stars, in-game money, the boats you own and the one you use, and the chosen language. You can delete this data by clearing the game's data or uninstalling it.

3. Device permissions

The game does not request any device permissions and does not use the internet.

4. Advertising

The current version shows no ads. If ads are added in a future version, they will come only from ad networks certified for Google Play's Families program, children will only be shown non-personalised ads, and this policy will be updated before that version is released.

5. Children

The game is suitable for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

6. Cookies and tracking

The game does not use cookies or tracking technologies.

7. Data security

Because the game sends no data to the developer or third parties, your data stays on your device.

8. Changes to this policy

We may change this privacy policy. Any changes will be published in this file.
